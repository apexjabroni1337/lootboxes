### ⚠️ Critical Legal & Ethical Considerations

Before integrating ANY gambling affiliate:

**FTC Compliance (Required)**
- All affiliate links must have clear "#ad" or "affiliate" disclosure
- Material connections must be disclosed prominently
- Cannot make misleading claims about winning odds

**Age Restrictions**
- Must include age verification warnings (18+ or 21+ depending on jurisdiction)
- Cannot target content to minors

**Valve Policy (Important — December 2025 Update)**
- Valve banned gambling site sponsorships at official tournament events
- This doesn't affect website affiliate programs but signals increased scrutiny

**Twitch/Platform Bans**
- Twitch banned gambling ad content in 2022
- If LootBoxes.com content appears on Twitch, gambling affiliate links must be removed from that content

**EU Loot Box Restrictions**
- Belgium and Netherlands have banned/restricted loot boxes
- Several EU countries considering similar legislation
- Geo-restrict gambling affiliate content for EU visitors if needed

**Recommendation:** Start with marketplace affiliates first. Add gambling affiliates only after consulting a lawyer about disclosure requirements for your specific jurisdictions. If you do add them, keep them clearly separated from the main CS2 content (e.g., a dedicated "/cs2/gambling" section with age gates and full disclosures).
