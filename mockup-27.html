Monetization Overview

Counter-Strike 2 operates a direct weapon case opening system requiring $2.49 keys in a cosmic lottery. Every pull yields a weapon skin, but the absence of pity mechanisms launches players into an infinite void of lower-rarity drops. The distribution heavily favors blue and light blue rarities, creating an event horizon where knife and glove drops exist in untouchable probability zones. This is a zero-guarantee system designed for extended commitment.
